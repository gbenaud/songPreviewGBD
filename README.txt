This addon allows you to listen to an extract of a song.  
Choose the start, duration or end and your choice will apply to all the songs in the list.
You can activate or not at any time

Works with: Media Monkey 5.0.4 (and may be before but I have not test that !)

## Installation  
--------------- 
Download the latest release from the releases section and double click the downloaded mmip file.  
A MediaMonkey dialog will automatically pop up, prompting you to confirm the installation.  


## Utilisation
---------------
With this addon you can choose to listen only a part of song. This applies to current list songs.

Open the Song Preview menu 
- on option bar
- on Play menu / Song Preview

Choose apply or not whith check box.  
Choose portion to listen with dropdown choice and values :  
- 'First Percentage'
- 'Time From Begin'
- 'Time From End'
- 'Time Period'
- '2nd Percentage'
- 'Length Percentage'

a basic sample is show to help you   

When song is playing, with a right click on Play/Pause button ( |> || ) on player, you can Enable or Disable preview.
The player button shows radio_button_partial when preview is enabled and radio_button_checked when it is disabled.
